## 1. Scope

This policy covers personal data that **Habeny Platform** receives from the Habeny
software and from our website, sales and support. It doesn't cover the data your
organization processes with Habeny on its own servers: there, your organization is the
controller, and Habeny Platform has no access to it. What the software stores locally,
and for how long, is described in Habeny's documentation (docs/privacy.md).
